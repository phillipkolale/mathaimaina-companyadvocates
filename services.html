Practice Specializations & Services Portfolio

Our legal units execute structured interventions across complex statutory mandates, property transfer regulations, and resolution systems in Kenya.

🔨

General Litigation & Complex Disputes

Our trial advocates represent clients across all levels of the Kenyan judicial system, including the Magistrates Courts, the High Court, the Court of Appeal, and the Supreme Court. We litigate complex commercial conflicts, constitutional petitions, employment challenges, and judicial review proceedings with meticulous attention to detail.

🤝

Arbitration and Specialized Tribunals

Protracted court battles aren't always ideal. Our certified arbitrators represent corporate interests in private domestic and international arbitrations, specialized mediation environments, and statutory bodies including the National Environment Tribunal, Public Procurement Administrative Review Board (PPARB), and Tax Appeals Tribunal.

🏛️

Banking Matters & Corporate Architecture

We provide legal advisory to tier-one financial institutions, microfinance bodies, and corporate investment groups. Our capabilities include structuring asset-backed securitizations, perfecting banking security charges, processing debentures, and handling regulatory compliance under the Banking Act and CBK directives.

In the corporate sphere, we guide clients through complex business setups, shareholder agreements, compliance reviews, mergers and acquisitions, and structural corporate dissolutions.

🏠

Conveyancing & High-Value Real Estate Law

Our real estate division provides end-to-end legal support for property transactions across Kenya. We oversee complex title searches, lease drafts, sectional title registrations, subdivisions, approvals, and swift property transfers under the Land Act.

🖋️

Commissioning, Notarization, & Recovery Services

As authorized Commissioners for Oaths and Notaries Public, we certify and authenticate affidavits, statutory declarations, power of attorney documents, and global certifications.

Additionally, our commercial debt recovery department implements persistent, legally compliant frameworks to recover outstanding debts while strictly protecting our clients' reputations.

Governance: Client Transparency & Communication Policy

🔒

The Mandate of Absolute Confidentiality

Client confidentiality is an absolute legal duty at our firm. All sensitive correspondence, commercial trade secrets, and ongoing legal filings are stored within localized, encrypted data pipelines. Access to matter data is restricted via multi-factor authentication protocols. Our IT systems undergo regular audits to ensure full compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act.

📅

Structured Monthly Progress Reporting

We eliminate uncertainty in legal management. Our firm delivers itemized monthly reports detailing every active case and transaction status, tracking upcoming court dates, application statuses, and next steps. You remain fully informed throughout the entire lifecycle of your legal matter.
